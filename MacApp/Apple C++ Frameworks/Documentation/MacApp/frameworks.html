Frameworks

Two major frameworks with two supporting frameworks comprise the MacApp product. Prior to MacApp Release 13, MacApp consisted of only one framework, called 'MacApp'.

With the introduction of MacApp Release 13 a number of major changes were made to the product leading to a new level of abstraction that made it possible to have a number of framework components that were more independent.

The two major frameworks comprising the MacApp product are:

  • ACS (Apple Class Suites), and
  • MacApp

The two supporting frameworks are:

  • Network, and
  • WASTE
